## Design Context

### Users
Mac owners using a Huawei HarmonyOS phone or foldable as an extra display, beginning
with Shonn's Mate XT 2. They want clear, full-screen reading and chat, easy USB
reconnection, portrait support and minimal setup. This context comes directly
from the creator's requests in this project conversation.

### Brand Personality
YouWo.ai: calm, useful, elegant. The creator requests a free app with a small,
optional one-time support payment and an elegant invitation after each session.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a restrained native macOS presentation, warm neutral surfaces and a quiet
green accent. Keep the desktop uninterrupted. Avoid loud donation appeals,
countdowns, confetti, subscriptions or claims of payment success without proof.

### Design Principles
- Demonstrate usefulness before asking for support.
- Ask after each qualifying session; Not now dismisses only that session.
- Give declining equal clarity and retain all functionality for free.
- Use only a verified YouWo-owned payment destination.
- Release materials must distinguish actual device evidence from illustrations
  and developer-preview availability from general distribution.
